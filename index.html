ABOUT ME

Who I am

Curious by nature. Driven to build.

I'm Haamid Muhammed, a student from Sri Lanka with a strong interest in technology, programming, leadership, and creative problem-solving.

I'm curious about how things work and enjoy exploring new ideas, learning new skills, and turning concepts into practical solutions. I especially enjoy working with technology and discovering how it can be used to solve problems, improve ideas, and create something meaningful.

I'm also interested in personal growth, leadership, communication, and creativity. I believe that being a good leader is not only about taking responsibility, but also about listening, learning, adapting, and helping others grow.

Throughout my journey, I've had the opportunity to take on different challenges, learn from new experiences, and continuously improve myself. Every project and experience teaches me something new and helps me understand more about what I want to achieve in the future.

I'm still at the beginning of my journey, but I'm focused on learning, building, experimenting, and becoming better every day. I want to continue developing my skills, exploring new fields, and using my knowledge to create things that have a real purpose.
